# Foods seed

`foods.tsv` holds 7793 foods with energy (kcal), protein, fat and carbohydrate per 100 g. It is built from the USDA
SR Legacy dataset (final release, April 2018) by `build_foods_seed.py`. Values are rounded to 2 decimal places.

Source: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central, SR Legacy, 2019.
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov. The data is published under CC0 1.0.
